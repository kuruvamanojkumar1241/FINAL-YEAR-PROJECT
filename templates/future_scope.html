Future Scope & Technological Innovations

Pioneering Non-Invasive Early Oncology Detection

While traditional lung cancer diagnostics rely on invasive biopsies or late-stage radiological imaging, our research integrates Volatile Organic Compound (VOC) Breath Gas Chromatography with Spiking Neural Networks (SNN) and Clinical GenAI. Explore the five key technological horizons under active development below.

1. IoT e-Nose Sensor Pod

Hardware Phase

Direct serial and Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) integration with physical electronic nose sensor arrays (MQ-135, TGS2602, and quartz crystal microbalance sensors).

  • Real-time telemetry stream of exhaled alveolar gas concentration.
  • Automated baseline drift correction & temperature compensation.
  • Handheld point-of-care (PoC) diagnostic breathalyzer form-factor.
Telemetry API specifications ready for Arduino & ESP32.

2. Neuromorphic Edge SNN

AI Architecture

Deploying Spiking Neural Networks (SNN) directly onto ultra-low-power neuromorphic hardware (Intel Loihi 2 and SynSense Speck chips).

  • Event-driven, asynchronous spike train processing matching human biology.
  • Over 95% reduction in power consumption compared to standard GPU inference.
  • Enables battery-operated, real-time edge screening in rural clinics.
Current SNN PyTorch architecture is export-ready for Lava framework.

3. 3D Volumetric DICOM / PACS

Hospital Protocol

Native support for clinical DICOM (.dcm) multi-slice thoracic CT scans and hospital PACS (Picture Archiving and Communication System) network queries.

  • 3D pulmonary nodule volumetric segmentation & growth velocity tracking.
  • HL7 and FHIR clinical messaging standards compliance.
  • Direct PACS workstation query/retrieve for hospital radiologists.
Seamless integration into radiology viewing suites.

4. Federated Privacy AI

Privacy & Security

Decentralized federated training allowing multi-hospital institutional collaboration without sharing raw patient scans or identifiable healthcare records.

  • Zero-trust differential privacy & encrypted model weight aggregation.
  • Full compliance with HIPAA, GDPR, and medical ethical data guidelines.
  • Immunity against single-center dataset bias and demographic skew.
Patient confidentiality preserved cryptographically.

5. Multi-Omics Fusion

Precision Oncology

Harmonizing breath VOC spectra with blood circulating tumor DNA (ctDNA) and genomic driver mutation profiles (EGFR, ALK, KRAS, PD-L1).

  • Personalized targeted drug recommendation & immunotherapy matching.
  • Detects stage-I microscopic malignancy prior to visible radiological mass.
  • Post-resection minimal residual disease (MRD) non-invasive monitoring.
Revolutionizes stage-I early intervention efficacy.
